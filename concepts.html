Core Concepts of AI

Explore the primary branches, algorithms, and categories that form modern Artificial Intelligence.

Fundamental AI Branches

Machine Learning

Allows computers to analyze data, identify patterns, and improve performance over time without explicit rules.

Deep Learning

Uses multi-layered artificial neural networks inspired by human brain structures to process complex unstructured data.

Natural Language Processing

Enables machines to read, process, translate, and synthesize human spoken and written languages naturally.

Computer Vision

Empowers computer systems to identify objects, classify scenes, and process context from digital images or videos.

Robotics

Combines AI software with physical mechanical devices to allow machines to perform automated actions independently.

Expert Systems

Software designed to emulate the decision-making skills of a human expert in specific knowledge domains.

Categorization of AI

Narrow AI (Weak AI)

AI designed for specific dedicated tasks like voice assistants or facial recognition. All current AI operates as Narrow AI.

General AI (Strong AI)

A theoretical level of AI where a machine exhibits human-like cognitive ability across any domain.

Super AI

A hypothetical stage where machine intelligence surpasses human capabilities across all creative, social, and analytical tasks.
